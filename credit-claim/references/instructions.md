**Before You Start**
Use a personal Gmail account. Corporate or school-managed accounts will NOT work. The domain MUST be `@gmail.com`. It is okay to create a new Gmail account, the credits will work.
You must have a phone to scan the QR code for 2-step authentication. This is to prevent fraudulent use of event credits and protect other people's ability to access the event from bots and other forms of account abuse.
Use Google Chrome in Incognito mode to prevent account conflicts.

**Step 1: Claim Credits**
Open a new Incognito window, paste your event link, and sign in with your personal Gmail.
Click below to copy your special event link:

https://me.developers.google.com/benefits/claim/gen-ai-apac-cloud-run-1

Participants must use an @gmail.com domain account to redeem credits; no corporate or personal domains will work, even if it says “eligible” in the UI. The redemption will fail.

Once the credits are redeemed, you will be able to see the Billing page.

You can either associate the new Billing account with a current project, or (preferably) can create a new project for the lab and it will 
automatically assume the new billing account.

We have controls in place to prevent fraudulent use of accounts, e.g. bots, cryptomining, impersonation. If there are suspicious signals, such as redeeming multiple accounts with one device or any sign of automation, we will block the credit redemption.

The Billing accounts will become unavailable for creation 3 ½ hours after the start of the event, and will last for 8 hours once they are created.
They are capped at $5 and will disappear (so no additional resources can be used) after the time expires.

Accept the Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service. Once applied, you'll see the message showing the credit has applied.

It may take up to five minutes to create the Billing Account, so don't worry if it doesn't appear instantly after the redemption is approved.

**Step 2: Environment Setup**
In the same Incognito window, go to:

console.cloud.google.com

Create a new project.

Navigate to the new project once it's created. (You may still be in an old project, check the name at the top of the console.)

The billing account will automatically be associated. You can check the billing name on the billing page. Make sure it has the right date.
